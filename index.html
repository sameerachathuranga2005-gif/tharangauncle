About Green Light Enterprises

Your Strategic Partner in
Reliable & Resilient Packaging.

Green Light Enterprises (GLE) is a trusted supplier of high-quality stretch film and industrial packaging solutions based in Ja-Ela, Sri Lanka. We are dedicated to delivering durable, dependable, and cost-effective packaging solutions that empower our customers to safeguard products, minimize transport breakage, and streamline packing floor operations.

With a steadfast commitment to strict quality control, prompt technical service, and long-term business partnerships, GLE supplies manufacturers, export leaders, and logistics providers across Sri Lanka.

Real World Applications

Different Industries. Same Goal:
Safe, Uncompromised Transport!

Our packaging materials are engineered to withstand the distinct mechanical demands of Sri Lanka's leading export and domestic manufacturing sectors.

Coir & Coco-Peat Exporters

High elongation holding force resists swelling and maintains compression across humid container shipments.

Rubber & Tire Factories

Heavy-duty tear resistance prevents puncture from heavy steel bead rims and heavy vulcanized blocks.

PVC & Plastic Makers

Surface scratch protection and tight bundling for conduit pipes, gutters, and architectural profiles.

Cable & Electrical

Dust and moisture barrier for copper coils, wooden drums, switchgear cartons, and electrical conduit.

Food & Beverage

Hygienic outer wrap preventing bottle tipping, carton crushing, and moisture contamination during transit.

Packaging & Logistics Hubs

Fast wrapping speeds and high load-retention for multi-SKU freight forwarders and 3PL warehouses.

Agriculture & Horticulture

UV-stabilized silage films and moisture-sealed bundling for organic produce and farm goods.

Concrete, Bricks & Timber

High-puncture film and heavy strapping for sharp paver corners, cement blocks, and timber packs.
